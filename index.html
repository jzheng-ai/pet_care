Which service should I choose?

Start with The Freshen Up for a bath and brush, or The Fluff & Buff if your pet also needs a trim. Use the visit planner to put together your preferences.

What if my pet is nervous?

Let the grooming team know before your visit. Share any sensitivities, previous grooming experiences, and anything that helps your pet feel comfortable.

What should I bring?

A secure leash or carrier, your pet’s care information, and details about any sensitivities. Confirm the shop’s vaccination requirements before your appointment.

Can I book an appointment here?

This is a sample shop homepage. The visit planner creates a summary on your device; it does not send a booking or reserve an appointment.
